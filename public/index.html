PAUSEESCOPTIONS
  • □ ○ △ each have their own string: keep pressing the same button for the next hit (□ 5 hits, ○ 4, △ 3). On a string's LAST hit you may switch once to another string (or ↑ + □ launch) — that second string always ends in a knockdown.
  • Too far away? The first hit dashes in by itself so it connects. STAMINA (green bar) is used by combos, dashes and jumps and refills after a short pause. An air dash toward the enemy flies straight at them, up or down.
  • Shiki Genkai work in the air too: you hang there while the technique plays out.
  • STAGES (pick one in the main menu): the arena is wider than the screen and the camera follows both fighters. SHINJUKU ROOFTOP drops off on both sides — fall off and you catch the edge, lose 5% HP and vault back up with a moment of invincibility. TUNNEL ARENA: a sand pit deep under the city, wooden walls, and a roaring crowd behind the glass.
  • SUNNY: ○ grabs (can't be blocked) — walk them around for a second, then ○ again headbutts them, □ drives their face into the floor or △ breaks them over her knee. △ once lifts them with a hook (follow up with □), △△ ends in the face breaker. SOLAR AURA sets anyone who touches her on fire. TWIN SUNS Lv3 burns every bar and her Shin needs a moment to come back.
  • ANZAI: his Shiki Genkai hit a little harder than everyone else's, above all at Lv1. △ punches into the smoke in front of him and the fists come out behind the opponent — only up close.
  • PARRY: tap block just before a hit lands. The attacker staggers, you heal 6% and can punish. Mashing block won't parry.
  • Hold ↑ or ↓ with K / L for the second technique of each Shiki Genkai. Kaito's SURGE powers up Phantom Rush. Itsuki's REGENERATION heals while it drains Shin; BRAWL can't be escaped once the first blow lands. Ikato's FLESH SPAWN creatures fight on their own for a few seconds. SHIN FORM (Lv 3): every hit echoes. Two attacks meeting = CLASH.
  • Combos break after 10 hits. A missed attack leaves you open; hitting someone mid-attack is a COUNTER (+20%, PUNISH +30%).
  • Jumping and dashing make you untouchable for a moment. Crouching dodges all but down attacks.
  • Shiki Genkai costs Shin bars ◆. Bars refill over time and on J / □ hits. Striped bars unlock at higher levels.
  • Your Shin level rises with the damage you deal: more damage, faster refill, stronger Shiki Genkai.
  • ULTRA: at Shin MAX with every bar full, press K + L (L1 + R1). It costs everything and drops you back to Shin Lv 2.
